## Misses

### 1. CLS on `/` (0.098–0.217) and `/?q=…` (0.240): **Must fix**

**Fixed in this PR** as below: `RecipeListPage` draws the recipes once the links have answered
(not measured again after the fix; the observer measured the same change in a scratch build).

- **Evidence:** `layout-shifts` names `main > div.mt-6` ("Search your recipes … Filter by
  tags"), the `ListFilters` block. The same build and data with the `imports` collection
  emptied gives CLS 0.000 and Performance 100 on both routes (`lh-m4-*-noimports-mobile.json`).
  The score depends on whether the two responses land in the same frame (0.098 vs 0.217 on
  `/`). It should grow with the number of links, since each row adds height (only 2 links were
  measured).
- **Cause:** `/` asks for `GET /api/recipes` and `GET /api/imports` at once.
  `RecipeListPage.tsx:77` draws `ListFilters` and the card placeholders straight away. Its
  condition `recipes?.length !== 0` is true while recipes are loading.
  `ImportList.tsx:45` draws nothing until its list arrives, then puts "Your links" and its rows
  above them, so everything below moves down. Performance drops to 88–89 because of this one
  shift; the other numbers are unchanged.
- **Fix (verified in a scratch copy of 9417f03):** in `RecipeListPage`, read
  `useQuery(importListQuery).isPending` (the query is already cached; no extra request) and draw
  everything after `<ImportList … />` (line 72) only once it's false. Result: CLS 0.000 and
  Performance 100 on `/` and `/?q=…` (2 runs each), LCP unchanged at 754 ms (the `<h1>` is
  above). The page shows its heading and link box first, then the links, filters and cards
  together. The other way, a placeholder for the links, can't know how many rows to reserve.

### 2. First-load JS: `/recipes/new` and `/imports/:id` 151.5 kB, `/recipes/:id` with its delete button 162.8 kB: **Must fix (or accept: a decision)**

**Decided (2026-11-07):** the user raised the SPA budget to 165 kB (metrics skill), on the case
below; no code change. Every route is within it.

- **Evidence:** the bundle table above. `/` is at 149.2 kB, 0.8 kB under.
- **Cause:** every route now carries the form stack (about 17 kB) in the entry, plus about
  3.5 kB of imports UI that only `/` and `/imports/:id` use. Most of every route is the libraries
  that `stack-conventions` require. Raw sizes from the source maps: react-dom 207 kB, zod 88.5,
  TanStack Form with its store 64, TanStack Query 36, react-router 38. In every split I tried,
  each route stayed between about 147 and 152 kB.
- **What I tried, in scratch builds of 9417f03** (sizes from the chunk graph, change against the
  same build):

  | Change                                                                       | `/`   | `/new` | `/:id` (+ delete) | `/imports/:id` | `/` mobile LCP |
  | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | ------ | ----------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
  | (a) `RecipeListPage` behind `lazy()`, its `<h1>` moved to the route in `App.tsx` | +2.3  | −2.8   | −20.4             | −2.8           | 0.75 s (=)     |
  | (a′) the same with the `<h1>` left inside the lazy page                      | +2.2  | −3.2   | −20.8             | −1.1           | **2.15 s** ✗   |
  | (b) `ReviewImportPage` behind `lazy()`                                       | +0.1  | +0.1   | +0.1              | +2.0           | —              |
  | (c) (b) + `ImportList` behind `lazy()`                                       | +1.5  | −0.4   | −0.4              | +1.5           | —              |

  (a) brings `/recipes/new`, `/imports/:id` and `/recipes/:id` with its delete button under
  150 kB (about 148.7, 148.7 and 142.4). It puts `/` about 1.5 kB over, at about 151.5, because
  the form stack becomes a shared chunk and splitting adds overhead. (a′) shows that the `<h1>`
  has to stay outside the lazy page. (b) and (c) don't help: rolldown splits shared code into
  new chunks.
- **What I recommend:** (a), the split M3 also proposed, plus a decision from the user on the
  last ~1.5 kB on `/`: accept it with a reason, or raise the SPA budget (for example to 160 kB)
  in the design doc. The case for raising it: (a) took 20.4 kB off `/recipes/:id` and its LCP
  moved 4 ms (1955 vs 1959 ms). That route's LCP is set by round trips (entry, then
  `GET /api/recipes/:id`), not by bytes. Fitting every route under 150 kB in code means a
  lighter library than the conventions allow (a native `<dialog>` instead of Radix would save
  13.6 kB on `/:id`; `zod/mini` might save some of the 25 kB of zod, not measured), or removing
  `@tanstack/devtools-event-client` (about 1.2 kB, needs an alias: fragile).

5. **`GET /api/imports` sends every ready import's whole draft:** 29.8 kB per 200 at the 50

   polls small once Gemini drafts are longer (M5).

   then `SORT` read 104 keys and 104 docs to return 1, as `indexes.ts` says. Fine for one user;
   note it in §4.3 if the queue can grow. Each worker also asks every second per loop when idle

7. **The e2e worker log loses its shutdown lines:** Playwright sends SIGTERM to the whole
   process group, so `tee` dies before the worker's "SIGTERM: finishing the jobs in hand".
   `playwright.config.ts:38` says so. If that line is wanted: `node src/worker.ts | (trap ''
   TERM; tee ../.metrics/e2e-worker.log)`. I ran this pipeline by hand, sent SIGTERM to the
   group, and the line was kept.
8. **Render-blocking CSS** (est. 150 ms) and **Agentic Browsing 48–50** (`llms-txt`,

## Polling (2 s while an import is in progress)

Measured with a browser on `/` and a link waiting for its retry:

- 10 `GET /api/imports` in 20 s: one every 2.0 s, 30 a minute per open tab. When nothing changed
  they are 304s (ETag; the browser sends `If-None-Match`), answered in 2–7 ms. Each one is still
  an indexed query (`IXSCAN(ownerId_1_createdAt_-1__id_-1)`, keys = links ≤ 50) and one log line
  (point 4 above).
- Hidden tab: 0 requests in 10 s (TanStack Query's `refetchIntervalInBackground` is off).
- How long it polls: with the fake extractor a link is ready in about 4 s (up to 1 s to be
  claimed, plus 3 s), so 2–3 polls. A link that fails polls through both backoffs (10 s, then
  60 s) and three attempts: about 80 s, so about 40 polls. With Gemini (M5) it polls for as long
  as extraction takes.
- Load: negligible here (one cook, ≤ 7 ms per poll, 304s). The cost to watch is the log volume
  (point 4).
