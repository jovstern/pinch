### Method note: first-load JS after F1–F4 and the UI polish (2026-11-10)

The canonical figure is **Vite's own gzip size** of each chunk (the build output), summed over
the entry and the chunks it imports, plus the route's lazy chunk and its imports: the table above.
The F2–F4 builders measured with `gzip -9` instead, which reads about 1.5–2 kB low on these
routes, so their PRs looked within budget when they weren't. Corrected, Vite's figures:

| Route group | `m8-f4-undo` (F4b) | polish, before its review fixes | after them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| App routes (`/recipes`, a recipe) | 179.5 | 179.0 | **160.5** (`/recipes`' Filters panel later, 163.2) |
| `/` Add recipe (with its paste box chunk) | 179.5 | 179.0 | **179.2** |
| Editor (write, edit, review) | 186.8 ❌ | 186.3 ❌ | **184.7** |
| Cook mode | 186.9 ❌ | 186.4 ❌ | **167.9** |

The fix, in the polish's review fixes: TanStack Form (~17 kB) was in the entry only for the paste
box, so the paste box is its own chunk (`LazyImportForm`), and so is the Filters panel from 1024 px
(`LazyFiltersPanel`), each with a fallback of its size. Add recipe waits for the queue before it
shows its centred block (no layout shift: CLS 0.25 → 0), and the queue is preloaded by
`index.html` and awaited before the first render on `/` (`main.tsx`), so the block paints with
the header. Lighthouse mobile, median of 3, against the local preview: `/` LCP **0.75 s** with an
empty queue and with six imports (it was 2.41 s on `m8-f4-undo`, over its 2.0 s budget: the empty
state waited on a request sent by the JS), CLS **0.000**; `/recipes` LCP 2.46 s, CLS 0.000
(desktop CLS 0.010). Making the whole page lazy instead read 2.6–3.0 s: React holds a lazy page
that arrives just after its fallback for 300 ms (its Suspense throttle), and Lighthouse adds the
chunk's round trip to the chain.

With the polish's second branch (the recipe count, the pot faces) the editor went 0.4 kB up, back
over by 0.1, so a failed card's Retry and Dismiss load on their own too (`ImportList`, with a
fallback of their row's height). Then: app routes **160.1**, `/` **178.9**, `/recipes` with its
panel **163.0**, editor **184.3**, cook **167.5** kB; `/` LCP 0.75 s, CLS 0.000 (desktop 0.62 s,
0.000). **Open:** `/recipes` LCP is 2.62 s (median of 3; `m8-f4-undo` 2.42, the first branch 2.47),
over its 2.5 s budget: the tab's count is one more request at start-up, and the local preview
serves HTTP/1.1, where Lighthouse queues it with the page's own. Without the count it reads
2.41 s; preloading it in `index.html` didn't help (2.61).

### The user's decisions (2026-11-10)

- **First-load JS budget raised**, rather than drop TanStack Form from the paste box (option 2) or
  `tailwind-merge` (option 3): ≤ 180 kB on the app's routes (175.4 measured), and **≤ 185 kB on the
  routes that add their own chunk** to the first load: the editor (write, edit, review; 181.4) and
  cook mode (182.9). The editor's budget was expected at ~180; it is 181.4 after lazy-loading its
  dialogs, and cook mode, which the decision didn't name, needs 182.9: one budget of 185 for both,
  rounded up to the next 5. Recorded in the metrics skill.
- **The review page's form chunk preloaded** (M7's option (a)): built and measured, then **left
  out**. A small Vite plugin wrote a `modulepreload` of the `RecipeForm` chunk into `index.html`,
  only on the editor's addresses. With the other fixes in, the review page was already 2.42 s, and
  the preload changed nothing there (2.421 → 2.419 s) while it made the edit page slower (2.27 →
  2.42 s): the chunk then competes with the entry for the first round trips, where before it came
  alongside the recipe's data (`usePreloadRecipeForm`). Nothing left to buy with it.

### What each fix did

1. **Filters' sheet on its first press** (`FiltersButton`, `FiltersSheet`): Radix's Dialog left the
   entry, −10.6 kB on every route. Opened as `DeleteRecipeDialog` is: open while on the page, focus
   back to the button by hand (no Radix Trigger in the first load).
2. **Toaster and Sonner with the first toast** (`useToastSent` in `lib/toasts.tsx`, Layout): they
   left every page's LCP graph. `dismissToast` does nothing until a toast has been sent, so the
   review page no longer fetches Sonner as it opens. Sonner's live region now appears with the
   first toast: its toast is added a tick after the region mounts (Sonner's own subscribe), which
   should still be announced; not yet checked with a screen reader.
3. **The ⋯ menu on intent, not at idle**: at idle it was still on the picture page's LCP path
   (fetched at 95 ms, done at 98, the picture painted at 123: 2.58 s). Now a pointer over ⋯ or
   focus on it starts the chunk, the press swaps the menu in, open. On the first keyboard open the
   menu itself takes the focus and ↓ reaches Edit (Radix sees the key only on its own trigger).
4. **Write a recipe's tip above the form**: 2.61 → 0.76 s. From 1024 px it now spans both columns
   instead of the left one.
5. **My recipes' first row eager** (the first two cards, a phone's row): `loading="eager"`,
   `fetchpriority="high"`.
6. **Add tag's sheet and the leave question on press**: the editor's first load −11.6 kB in all
   with 1. A sheet or question whose chunk won't load just doesn't open
   (an `ErrorBoundary` with `fallback={null}` around it), so a failed chunk never replaces the
   form and what's typed. Cancel, like Discard, is now `aria-disabled` while the recipe saves, so
   it can't open "Discard your changes?" over a save in flight.
7. **Preload of the form chunk**: left out (above).
8. **⋯ menu not modal**, and not portalled: a non-modal menu outside every landmark is axe's
   "region"; inside the page it is clean. Tab still can't leave it (Radix), Escape and a press
   outside close it, and focus goes back to ⋯.

Not re-measured in this wave: desktop Lighthouse (100 everywhere before, and nothing added), API
latency, the 56-scan ad-hoc axe sweep (the two states it failed are now in the e2e spec).

Causes, each with its share:

1. **Requests in the LCP's graph that the page doesn't need to paint** (−175 to −190 ms):
   Lighthouse ties the LCP to every request that ends before it in the trace. The Toaster and
   Sonner chunks (`Layout.tsx:12`, `lib/toasts.tsx`) are fetched as the app starts, and
   `RecipeMenu` (`LazyRecipeMenu.tsx:13`) as the recipe shows; both finish before the LCP paint
   (trace: `RecipeMenu` 86–93 ms, LCP 122 ms), so both are on its path. On `vite preview`
   (HTTP/1.1, 6 connections) they also queue behind the 8 entry files.
   **Fix:** start the Toaster and Sonner after the page is up (`requestIdleCallback`, or on the
   first toast), and `RecipeMenu` on intent (pointer over / focus on ⋯) or at idle.
2. **`/recipes/new` paints its largest text from the lazy form** (−1.85 s): since R8a the page's
   `h1` is `sr-only` (`NewRecipePage.tsx:38`), so the LCP is the intro (`NewRecipePage.tsx:42`),
   rendered inside `LazyRecipeForm`: HTML → JS → `RecipeForm` chunk.
   **Fix:** render the intro above `<LazyRecipeForm>` (measured: 0.76 s).
3. **More main-thread work before the paint** (≈ +310 ms left after 1): observed (unthrottled)
   main-thread work on the recipe page is 378 ms vs 257 ms pre-M8, scripting in the entry 130 vs
   69 ms; Lighthouse multiplies it by 4. A CPU profile at 4× (5 runs, by source package) puts the
   growth in `react-dom` (27.5 → 50.7 ms: more components), parse/compile (75 → 123 ms),
   `tailwind-merge` (13.8 ms, new), Zod (28 → 39 ms), Sonner (5.3 ms, new). No single fix; 1
   and option 3 above help a little.
4. **`/recipes`' LCP is now a card's picture** (`RecipeCard`, `loading="lazy"`,
   `fetchpriority="auto"`; `lcp-discovery-insight` fails): HTML → JS → list → picture. Making the
   first row `loading="eager"` is right but still needs that fourth round trip.

On HTTP/2 (a small TLS server over the same builds, as a host would serve them): M8 is 1.81 s on
the recipe page (1.95 s with a picture) and 1.81 s on `/recipes/new`, against 1.51 s and 0.76 s
before M8: inside both budgets in production, but the budget is measured on `vite preview`.

**Recommendation (Must fix):** 1 + 2 (≈ 25 lines) bring every route but `/imports/:id` under its
budget, with 26–85 ms to spare. `/imports/:id` (2.57 s) also waits for `RecipeForm` and the
picture; it would need the form's chunk preloaded from `index.html` (M7's option (a)) or accept
the miss.

## Miss 3: axe, the recipe's ⋯ menu open

16 violations in 56 scans, all in one state: the ⋯ menu open on `/recipes/:id` (every width and
theme): `aria-hidden-focus` (serious: `header`, `main`), `landmark-one-main`,
`page-has-heading-one`, `region` (`div[data-radix-popper-content-wrapper]`). Radix's modal
