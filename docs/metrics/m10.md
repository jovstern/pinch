Measured 2026-11-13 on `m10-i10-unit-combobox`, commit **b96ddab** (origin/master + I10, the unit's
combobox, and the end-of-milestone e2e), in a worktree of its own (`npm ci`, then `vite build
--manifest`). The app ran on its own ports: the API on 3330 (`TRUST_PROXY=1`, `EXTRACTOR=fake`), the
fake-extractor worker, and `vite preview` on 4330. The database was `pinch-test-obs-04e8bdda`
(indexes from `dbIndexes.ts`), dropped afterwards. The dev servers (3000, 3001, 5173), the user's
worker and the `pinch` database were not touched. No Gemini call was made, and no `.env` file was
read.

**Data.** Two users, signed up through `POST /api/auth/signup` with `language`:
- **E** (`en`): the 10 samples (`seed.ts`, one of them Hebrew: "סלט ירקות") and two screenshot
  recipes with pictures (ffmpeg `testsrc2` + `noise`, 1280×720 and 1080×1920, made into 400×225 and
  281×500 thumbnails by the fake worker), approved with English text. The measured recipe is
  "Shakshuka for a slow Saturday", with the landscape picture.
- **H** (`he`): the same 10 samples, 3 Hebrew ones more, and the same two pictures approved with
  Hebrew text ("שקשוקה של שבת", "עוגת שמרים שוקולד").

Both queues are empty, so `/` is the text-first page (2.0 s budget).

**The language, and how the cookies reached Lighthouse.** Lighthouse 13.6.0 through its Node API,
as m09's fixes: a fresh headless Chrome per run (`--lang=en-US`), the cookies set in its jar
(puppeteer: `pinch_session`, `pinch_signed_in`, `pinch_lang`), `disableStorageReset`. The jar, unlike
`--extra-headers`, reaches `document.cookie`, so `index.html`'s script reads `pinch_lang`. On top of
that, the English runs ran with `TZ=UTC` and the Hebrew ones with `TZ=Asia/Jerusalem` (the language
trap, metrics skill step 3). **Checked in `network-requests`:** none of the 28 English loads fetched
`he-*.js`; all 28 Hebrew loads did. Playwright confirmed `<html lang dir>`: `en ltr` / `he rtl` on
every page. Mobile is the median of 3 runs, desktop 1 run, simulated throttling.

**First-load JS** is the sum of Vite's own gzip figures for the entry plus the route's lazy chunk
and its imports, from the build manifest (never `gzip -9`). The chunks each route really fetched were
checked in Lighthouse's `network-requests`: they match.

"Change" is against **m09-fix** (the last measured state, `docs/metrics/m09.md`).

## The language switch (5 users, en → he → en, Playwright, unthrottled)

`PATCH /api/auth/me` answers 200 in 5–22 ms (the browser's `responseEnd`). The reload starts 4–6 ms
after the answer; `load` fires 9–12 ms after that; the Hebrew `h1` ("המתכונים שלי") is drawn 40 ms
after `load` (the `he-*.js` chunk joins the first render, `main.tsx`), the English one 23 ms after.
From the answer to the new language on screen: **50–55 ms to Hebrew, 35–40 ms to English**. No
console message on any of the 10 switches. (The time from the click to the PATCH, 375–836 ms in my
script, is Playwright waiting for the menu's open animation to settle before it clicks, not the app.)

A new device costs one reload (§16.1): a Hebrew account opened with the device's cookie at `en`
draws `/recipes` at 2.85 s and a recipe at 2.61 s under Lighthouse's mobile throttling, against 2.50
and 2.39 s on a device that already says Hebrew (+0.35 and +0.22 s). It happens once per device,
and stays under 3.0 s.

## Worth doing (within budget)

1. **Hebrew's first load is 4.8 kB more than the budget counts, and over 210 kB on three routes.**
   §16.10 defines the budget on the English entry (Hebrew is "a lazy chunk, fetched only in
   Hebrew"), so nothing here misses as written. But a Hebrew user's first load is the entry *and*
   `he-*.js` (4.8 kB), which the first render waits for:

   | Route | en | he |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | app routes | 181.8 | 186.6 |
   | `/` | 200.7 | 205.5 |
   | cook | 189.5 | 194.3 |
   | editor | 208.2 | **213.0** |
   | `/login` · `/signup` | 207.1 · 206.9 | **211.8 · 211.7** |

   It doesn't show in the LCP: Hebrew's figures are within 0.03 s of English's (the chunk is fetched
   beside the route's own, not after it). **For the user to decide** whether the budget counts the
   Hebrew first load. If it does, the lever is the English strings, which a Hebrew user downloads in
   the entry and never uses (`en.ts`, about 6.5 kB gzip as source, estimated; not isolated): loading
   them as Hebrew is, and inlining only the fallback's few strings, would put both languages at
   about the same size. That changes §16.3, so it is a design decision, not a fix.
   **Decided (the user, 2026-11-13):** the budget stays on the English entry, as §16.10 defines
   it; moving the English strings out of the entry is on `docs/progress.md`'s "After M10" list.
2. **Cook's LCP is 2.455 s (en) and 2.478 s (he) of 2.5**; one English run was 2.473. It gained
   0.16–0.18 s in M10, as did edit (+0.16–0.19) and the recipe page (+0.11–0.13): the entry grew by
   25 kB (i18next, react-i18next, the English strings), and the LCP's element render delay is the
   entry's download and run under the throttling (`lcp-breakdown-insight`: TTFB 2 ms, element render
   delay 432–438 ms observed). **Measured and ruled out:** starting the recipe's request with the
   chunk rather than inside it (`useQuery(recipeQuery(id))` in `LazyCookPage`, the way
   `EditRecipePage` does) gave 2.459 s (en) and 2.483 s (he), the same. The request does start at
   91 ms instead of 385 ms, so cook's data is not the bottleneck; the entry is. Any new string or
   library in the entry pushes cook over 2.5 s first. The only levers are bytes out of the entry
   (Worth doing 1's English strings would be one), or accepting it (on HTTP/2, as a host serves it,
   m08 measured about −0.8 s on these pages).
3. **The editor's JS is at 208.2 of 210** (the auth pages 207.1, `/` 200.7). The next string added
   to the entry (every slice adds some, §16.10) or to `RecipeForm` crosses it.
4. **Cook mode asks for its recipe 300 ms after its chunk arrives** (unthrottled, observed: chunk
   done at 89 ms, `GET /api/recipes/:id` at 385 ms, the same in Hebrew). The edit page asks at 104
   ms, beside its chunk. It doesn't change the LCP today (above), so I did not trace the gap (the
   `chrome-devtools` MCP did not connect). In the variant above, the early request was followed by a
   second `GET /api/recipes/:id` at 408 ms, so whatever runs then also refetches.
5. **One slow read in the e2e log: `GET /api/recipes/:id` 304 in 531 ms**, in the middle of the
   parallel tests (a login at 69 ms, `/api/imports` at 99 and 119 ms around it). The same read is
   3 ms elsewhere in the log and p95 10.5–12.7 ms in my benchmark, so it is the e2e's load (the
   tests run side by side, with argon2 and the videos), not the route. The budget is the p95, which
   is met; noted because it is the slowest request seen.
6. Carried from m09: Vite warns that the entry is over 500 kB before minification and gzip (579.4
   kB; 181.8 kB gzip is what the budget counts); `/api/recipes/count` is the read with the widest
   tail (p95 13 ms); the leftover databases `pinch-test-00067fb7-…`, `pinch-test-ab7a1d4b-…`, and
   now `pinch-test-204762c8-…`, `pinch-test-89232fdb-…` and `pinch-test-ui-cbecfd40` (not mine; the
   last may belong to a running review).
