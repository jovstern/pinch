# UX pass — M5 (Gemini)

The designer's first pass, 2026-11-08, on branch `m5-end` (HEAD `4943a91`). There was no
`docs/ux/` before it, so this pass covers the whole app as built through M5, not only what M5
changed. It is the evidence behind the first `plan.md`; the method is
`.claude/skills/ux-strategy/SKILL.md`.

**Setup.** The production build (`vite preview`, port 4210) against its own API (3210) and
worker (`EXTRACTOR=fake`) on a throwaway `pinch-ux-m05` database: the 10 seeded recipes plus
one long real-world-shaped recipe (14 ingredients, 7 steps, a description), a YouTube link
and a pasted Hebrew text that became drafts, and an Instagram link that failed its three
tries. Every screen at 360, 768 and 1440 px; the empty state by mocking the two list
responses. Screenshots and the script in `.metrics/ux/m05/` (git-ignored): `<screen>-<width>.png`
is the first screenful, `-full.png` the whole page.

## What the app is today, in one line

A correct, accessible, well-engineered **form** with a list under it. It is not yet a place a
cook wants to open: nothing in it looks like food, the recipes are the last thing on the home
page, and the one screen a cook uses with wet hands (the recipe) is a document to scroll, not a
tool to cook from.

## What hurts, by screen

### `/` — "Your recipes" (home-imports-360.png, home-imports-360-full.png, home-imports-1440.png)

1. **The recipes are below the fold on every device.** At 360 px the first card starts about
   1,000 px down: heading, an empty 96 px textarea, a hint, Pinch it, "Your imports" (three
   dashed boxes), search, "Filter by tags", then the list. The page is named "Your recipes" and
   shows a form. With three imports the page is 2,151 px tall before "Show more" exists; at
   1440 px (home-imports-1440.png) a 900 px screen shows no recipe at all.
2. **The capture box is a big blank.** An empty multi-line textarea with a two-line hint and a
   left-aligned button reads as a form to fill, not a slot to drop a link into. The pasted link
   is one line; the box is four. On a phone, pasting means: tap the box, long-press, Paste,
   find the button. Four steps where one ("Paste") is possible.
3. **The cards are eleven identical white bars** (title, "4 ingredients · 30 min"). No image,
   no cuisine, nothing that says *new* or *cooked*; the list has no rhythm and no reason to
   scroll it for an idea. A Hebrew title is the only visual variety. At 1440 px the bars are
   720 px wide and 100 px tall, in one column, with 360 px of empty surface on each side.
4. **"Ready to review" doesn't stand out.** The one import waiting for the user is a dashed box
   with a secondary button, visually weaker than the recipe cards below it; a raw URL with its
   tracking parameters (`?igsh=MzRlODBiNWFlZA==`) is the biggest text in it
   (home-imports-1440.png). The failure state (home-failed-360-full.png) is fine in words
   ("We couldn't turn this into a recipe.", Retry, Dismiss) but the same weight as everything.
5. **Search and filters are in the middle of the page and far from their result.** The search
   box sits under the imports; with the filters open (home-filters-open-360-full.png) 35 chips
   in five groups push the list 1,700 px down; a filtered result (home-filtered-360-full.png:
   "1 recipe matches." then Mujadara) lands about 1,850 px from the top, and the "No recipes
   match" message (home-no-match-360.png) is not on the first screen at all. The filters are
   also the only part of the UI with colour-on-selection, so when one is ticked the page's
   strongest colour is a filter chip.
6. **Two ways to write a recipe, both weaker than the box.** "New recipe" in the header on
   every list screen, and "Write a recipe" in the empty state (home-empty-360.png). The header
   button has the same weight as the brand. The empty state itself is honest but gives a
   first-time user nothing to look at and no example of what a pinched recipe becomes.

### `/recipes/:id` — a recipe (recipe-long-360-full.png, recipe-long-1440.png, recipe-hebrew-360.png)

7. **It's a document, not a cooking tool.** 16 px body in one column; the 14 ingredients and 7
   steps of the long recipe are 1,761 px of scroll at 360 px. No step focus, no way to tick an
   ingredient off, no timer from "20 minutes", the screen locks mid-step (no wake lock), and
   the two most dangerous controls on the page, **Edit and Delete, sit above the ingredients**
   in the spot a thumb reaches first. At 1440 px (recipe-long-1440.png) half the width is empty
   while ingredients and steps queue in one column.
8. **The source is lost.** The recipe stores where it came from (`source.url`, §6) and the page
   never shows it: no "watch the reel" link, no platform mark. The cook cannot check the video
   when a step is unclear, which is exactly when they would.
9. **A Hebrew recipe reads half backwards.** The ingredient lines (`3 עגבניות`) and steps are
   left-aligned in an LTR page with English headings ("Ingredients", "Steps") and list markers
   on the left (recipe-hebrew-360.png). The boxes on the forms got `dir="auto"` in M5; the read
   view didn't.
10. **Nothing to look at.** No thumbnail (M6 brings one), no cuisine mark, the tags are grey
    pills. The ≈ nutrition line is good and quiet.

### `/imports/:id` — Review the recipe (review-360-full.png, review-1440.png)

11. **Review is the New recipe form with a different heading.** 2,610 px at 360 px for a
    3-ingredient draft; the real recipes in the spike had 10–19 ingredients, so a real review
    will be 5,000+ px (edit-long-360-full.png is 5,597 px for 14). The cook came to *check* the
    AI's work and is given 30 empty-looking inputs, the 35 tag chips open, and no view of what
    the AI read: the caption is not shown, the video is a raw link, nothing marks a field the
    model was unsure of (an empty Prep, a missing quantity).
12. **The decisions are at the bottom, under the chips.** Save recipe / Review later / Discard
    are 2,500 px down; Discard floats alone at the right (review-360-full.png). Nutrition, which
    the draft carries, isn't shown here at all, so the first time the cook sees it is after
    saving.

### `/recipes/new`, `/recipes/:id/edit` (new-360-full.png, edit-long-360-full.png, new-1440.png)

13. **Each ingredient is four labelled boxes and a Remove button**, stacked 2-high at 360 px
    (Amount · Unit · Ingredient / Note · Remove). Fourteen ingredients become 56 inputs and 14
    buttons. At 1440 px the Serves / Prep / Cook inputs are 240 px wide for two-digit numbers,
    and Amount and Unit are 80 px for "3 1/2" and "cloves" (new-1440.png).
14. **All 35 tag chips, always open**, on every form; the model already picks tags, so on the
    review page the cook is scanning 35 to confirm 2.

### The frame (every screen)

15. **The look is a default.** Warm off-white, one burnt orange, the system sans, 8–12 px
    radii, 1 px borders on everything: every element is a bordered rectangle, so nothing has
    hierarchy except size. There is no mark, no image, no texture, and no dark mode
    (`color-scheme: light`): at night, the time most reels are saved, the app is a white page.
    It is clean and passes every gate (metrics m04: 100 accessibility, 0 axe), and it would be
    hard to tell apart from a Tailwind starter.
16. **Navigation is a header link and a button.** No "where am I" except the heading; the
    "Not ready to review" page (review-not-ready-360.png) and the forms lose the header button,
    so the frame changes shape between screens.

## What is genuinely good (keep it)

- The engineering of every state: loading placeholders the size of the cards, errors that say
  what happened and offer the retry, focus that goes where the next action is, live regions
  that announce one change at a time, `aria-disabled` so focus never drops. Metrics m04:
  100 / 100 / 100, 0 axe violations, LCP 0.75 s. The new look must not lose any of this.
- The copy's voice: short, plain, in the cook's words ("Pinch it", "Ready to review", "We
  couldn't turn this into a recipe.", "Keep it").
- One textarea for link or text with nothing to choose (M5) is the right instinct for capture;
  the proposal below keeps it and makes it smaller and smarter.
- The ≈ nutrition line, the recipe's meta line ("Serves 4 · Prep 15 min · Cook 45 min") and
  the muted ingredient notes are quiet and right.
- Filters in the URL, replace-not-push, Back leaves the list: correct and worth keeping under
  any new filter UI.

## Proposals this pass adds

All new (first pass), all `proposed`; the ranked list with impact and effort is in `plan.md`:
U1 (home as a library, capture as a bar), U2 (cook mode), U3 (review as checking, not
typing), U4 (the look: tokens, type, mark, dark), U5 (recipe cards with a face), U6 (the
import queue as a status strip), U7 (filters as one row + a sheet), U8 (PWA: installable,
share target, Paste), U9 (want to cook / cooked), U10 (the source on the recipe), U11 (RTL
on the read view), U12 (shorter forms).
