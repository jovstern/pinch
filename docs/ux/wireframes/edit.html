Edit a recipe

Pass 02 · revision 2 (UX only, shadcn greyscale). One page for three jobs: reviewing a draft Pinch made from a link (/imports/:id), writing a recipe by hand (/recipes/new), and editing a saved one (/recipes/:id/edit). The fields are the same as the recipe itself (design §6). Only the top, the marks and the Save button change. It replaces the "check" screen from the first draft.

Phone · 360 · write a recipe by hand
  1. Breadcrumbs say where this page belongs: a draft and a hand-written recipe sit under New recipe, so that tab stays selected. Editing a saved recipe sits under My recipes.
  2. The draft's banner (shadcn Alert): what Pinch did, how many fields to check, and the way back to the video. The count goes down as you check them.
  3. A ◆ mark sits on a field the model was unsure of: a dashed border, a "Check" badge, and the reason in one line ("The video says 'a can'"). Changing the field, or tapping Looks right, clears it. Marks don't block Save; they ask for a look.
  4. Picture and title first. A draft uses the reel's cover; by hand, the dish's emoji until you add a photo. Change picture is the same on all three.
  5. Serves, Prep, Cook in one row, as numbers (the keyboard shows digits). Tags: chips with ✕, and Add tag opens a Command list grouped by facet (cuisine, meal, diet, flavor, fit).
  6. Ingredients as rows: amount · unit · name (the recipe's own shape, design §6), with a drag handle and ✕. Rows fit on one line at 360 px. Paste a list turns pasted lines into rows, for anyone copying from a note.
  7. Steps: numbered textareas that grow with their text, with a handle and ✕. Add step is under the last one.
  8. The Save bar is pinned to the bottom, in thumb reach, on every length of recipe: the secondary action (Discard / Cancel) and the one primary (Save).
  9. By hand: a tip at the top points to the faster way. The source says "Written by you" with an optional link. The placeholders are examples ("e.g. 200 g flour"), not instructions.
Desktop · 1440 · review a draft
  1. Two columns: what you edit most (title, ingredients, steps) on the left, 720 px; everything about the recipe (picture, source, details, tags) and Save on the right. The right column stays in view as you scroll.
  2. The banner names every marked field as a link, which jumps to it.
Phone · Save with something missing
Phone · Discard draft (AlertDialog)
  1. Missing a required field (a title, one ingredient, one step: design §6): Save scrolls to the first one, focuses it, and shows the message under it in words that say what to do ("Name the recipe."). A line above the Save bar counts what is left.
  2. Leaving with changes (Discard, Cancel, a breadcrumb, or Back) asks once: "Discard this draft?" Discarding shows a toast with Undo.

What the user sees first: the banner (draft) or the title field (by hand). Primary action: Save.
